# Income Tax Brackets & Rates (2026)

## Box 1: Wages and business profits

### Bracket 1

**Rate:** 35.75%

**Applies to:** The first tranche of taxable income after all deductions (zelfstandigenaftrek, startersaftrek, MKB winstvrijstelling, etc.).

**Confidence:** Medium — one explicit source; verify against Belastingdienst before final use. This is a critical filing input.

---

## Box 2: Deemed dividend from own company

### Two-tier structure

| Taxable income | Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to €68,843 | 24.5% |
| Above €68,843 | 31% |

**Who:** Directors-major shareholders (DGA) earning profit from their own BV/NV.

**How:** If you own >5% of a company and draw profit, the deemed or actual dividend is taxed in Box 2 at these progressive rates.

**Note:** Excess borrowing from your own company above €500,000 (excl. mortgages) is also taxed as Box 2 income.

**Confidence:** High

## Box 3: Wealth tax (capital/savings)

### Asset-class percentages (2026 provisional)

| Asset type | Percentage applied | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bank savings | 1.28% | Savings accounts, deposits |
| Investments & other assets | 6.00% | Stocks, bonds, real estate (not primary residence) |
| Debts | 2.70% | Mortgages, loans (deducted from asset base) |

**How it works:**
1. Sum all assets (financial + property, excl. primary home)
2. Subtract all debts
3. Apply these provisional percentages to calculate notional income
4. Tax that notional income at ~32% combined rate

### Tax-free allowances (2026)

| Status | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Single person | €59,357 |
| Married/registered partner | €118,714 (total; may split) |

**Example:** Single, €150,000 in savings. Allowance €59,357. Taxable wealth = €90,643. Apply 1.28% = €1,160 notional income → tax ≈ €370.

**Confidence:** High

### Volatility note

These percentages are **provisional** and change annually. They are set by the government based on average investment returns. Do not assume 2026 values hold for 2027.

## Innovatiebox: reduced corporate-tax rate on qualifying IP profits

**Effective rate:** 9% VPB on qualifying innovation profits (vs. standard VPB rates).

**Who:** BV/NV (corporations) with R&D-derived IP — patents, software, qualifying trademarks/processes — used in the business.

**How:** Isolate "innovation income" (profit attributable to qualifying IP) and tax that slice at 9% rather than the standard VPB brackets.

**Requirement:** Requires an S&O (WBSO) statement from RVO as nexus evidence, separate innovation return, and admin showing the link between R&D spend and resulting profit.

**Distinct from WBSO:** Innovatiebox = reduced VPB rate on IP profits. WBSO = wage-tax credit for R&D personnel costs. They often co-exist but are different regimes administered differently.

**Confidence:** High (rate); medium (year-versioned thresholds/details — verify per filing year)

**Cross-link:** `02-tax-opportunities/wbso-innovatiebox.md` covers both regimes in detail.

## Combined marginal rate (employee + employer)

For context (not directly filed):

- **Wages (Box 1):** 35.75% income tax + 8% employee social security ≈ **43.75% combined**
- **Corporate profits (Box 1 self-employed):** 35.75% income tax + 20.6% entrepreneur social security ≈ **56% combined** (before deductions)

This is why deductions (KIA, zelfstandigenaftrek, MKB winstvrijstelling) are so valuable for ZZP.
