# VAT Rates & Rules (2026)

## Standard VAT rates

| Category | Rate | Examples |
|----------|------|----------|
| Standard | 21% | Most goods, restaurant meals |
| Reduced | 9% | Food (unprepared), books, newspapers, childcare |
| Zero | 0% | Export, some medical/educational supplies |
| Exempt | — | Financial services, insurance, rent (not hospitality) |

**Confidence:** High (unanimous across all sources)

## Key change for 2026

**Logies (accommodation) moved to 21% rate** (previously lower).

Logies includes: hotel rooms, holiday rentals, camping, bed-and-breakfast. If you provide accommodation services, charge 21% VAT from 1 Jan 2026 onwards.

**Confidence:** High

## KOR: small business exemption

**Threshold:** Turnover ≤ **€20,000** per year

If you are a sole trader (ZZP) or partnership (VOF) with annual turnover not exceeding €20,000, you are exempt from VAT registration and do not charge/reclaim VAT.

- **Eligibility:** ZZP, VOF, eenmanszaak
- **Applies to all activities** — no sectoral exclusions
- **If exceeded:** Register for VAT in the same year or next; no retroactive charging allowed without explicit notice

**Confidence:** High

## On-premises food & drink: non-deductible input tax

**Rule:** BTW paid on food and drink **consumed on your business premises** is NOT deductible as input tax.

**Examples:**
- Coffee/snacks for staff: non-deductible
- Lunch provided to employees: non-deductible
- Client entertainment (meals, drinks): non-deductible
- Catering for a company event: non-deductible
- Staff restaurant costs: non-deductible

**Exception:** Food/drink purchased for **resale** (e.g., a restaurant or caterer selling to customers) is fully deductible.

**Why:** This is treated as private benefit or general consumption, not a direct business input.

**Confidence:** High

---

## Reverse-charge rule (verlegd)

For certain services (primarily EU cross-border services and import of goods), the VAT liability is **shifted to the buyer** (reverse-charge). The supplier does not invoice VAT; the buyer self-assesses.

- **Common case:** You buy services from an EU supplier; you owe VAT to Dutch authorities, not the supplier
- **VAT return:** Declare both input VAT (yourself) and output VAT (yourself) on same line if applicable

**Confidence:** High (standard EU rule)

## Invoice requirements (deductibility & compliance)

To deduct input VAT, every invoice must contain:

| Field | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Supplier name & address | Legal name as registered in KvK |
| Supplier VAT ID | Dutch: NL + 12 digits; EU: country code + ID |
| Invoice date | Date of issue |
| Unique invoice number | Sequential, not repeated |
| Description of supply | What was delivered / service rendered |
| Supply date | When goods/services provided (may differ from invoice date) |
| Unit price & quantity | Clear, itemized |
| **Ex-VAT amount** | Price before VAT |
| **VAT rate & amount** | e.g., "21% VAT: €21.00" |
| **Invoice total** | Inc. VAT |

**Missing or incorrect:** Invoice is not valid for VAT deduction. Belastingdienst will disallow the input VAT claim.

**Confidence:** High

## Suppletie: VAT correction form

**When:** You discover a VAT error (overcharged, undercharged, missed deduction, overstated deduction).

**Threshold:** Corrections **> €1,000** must be filed as a formal suppletie (aanvullingsdocument).

**Deadline:** **8 weeks from discovery** of the error. File with Belastingdienst.

**Smaller corrections (≤ €1,000):** Can be processed in your next regular VAT return, or filed as suppletie — your choice.

**Penalty if late:** Interest (wettelijke rente: ~6–8% p.a.) + potential admin fine.

**Example:** You discover in March that you missed €2,500 in deductible input VAT from January. File suppletie by end of May.

**Confidence:** High

---

## Quarterly vs. monthly VAT filing

**Standard:** Quarterly filing (end of month following quarter: 30 April, 31 July, 31 October, 31 January).

**Monthly:** If you have large VAT refunds or specific circumstances, Belastingdienst may require monthly filing.

Check your VAT registration notice (aangifteregeling) to confirm your frequency.

**Confidence:** High
