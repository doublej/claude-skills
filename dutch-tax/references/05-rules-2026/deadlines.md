# 2026 Tax Deadlines & Reporting Timeline

Master calendar of all major Dutch tax filing and administrative deadlines.

---

## VAT filing deadlines

### Quarterly filers (most common for small business)

| Quarter | Period | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Jan 1 – Mar 31 | **30 April** |
| Q2 | Apr 1 – Jun 30 | **31 July** |
| Q3 | Jul 1 – Sep 30 | **31 October** |
| Q4 | Oct 1 – Dec 31 | **31 January** (next year) |

**Submission:** Via DigiD login at Belastingdienst.nl or via accountant.

**Frequency check:** Confirm your VAT registration notice (aangifteregeling) specifies quarterly vs. monthly filing.

**Confidence:** High

---

## VAT correction (suppletie)

| Trigger | Deadline | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Error discovered | Within **8 weeks** | File suppletie form with Belastingdienst |
| Amount > €1,000 | Formal suppletie required | Submit immediately |
| Amount ≤ €1,000 | Next regular VAT return or suppletie | Your choice |

**Penalty if late:** Interest accrues (~6–8% p.a. wettelijke rente) + potential admin fines.

**Form:** Aanvullingsdocument VAT (online via Belastingdienst portal).

**Confidence:** High

---

## Income tax filing (annual return)

### Standard deadline

**31 May** — File annual income tax return (Aangifte inkomstenbelasting) for prior tax year.

**Example:** For 2025 tax year, file by 31 May 2026.

**Extensions:** May be granted if you can demonstrate hardship; request in writing before deadline.

**Confidence:** High

---

## Corporate tax (Vpb) filing

### For corporations (BV/NV)

**Deadline:** **9 months after fiscal year-end** (typically 30 September for calendar-year companies).

**Requirements:**
- iXBRL format filing (mandatory 2026)
- Annual financial statements
- Corporate tax return
- Accountant sign-off (if turnover > threshold)

**Confidence:** High

---

## Annual accounting & accountant sign-off

| Step | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Prepare annual accounts (balance sheet, P&L) | Before submission |
| Accountant review/audit (if required) | Before filing |
| File with KvK (company registry) | With tax return or within 8 weeks of year-end |
| Tax return submission to Belastingdienst | Per income tax / corporate tax deadlines above |

**Accountant requirement:** Mandatory if BV/NV turnover exceeds ~€8 million (consult accountant on thresholds).

**Filing:** Via KvK/Belastingdienst portal (iXBRL format for 2026 onward).

**Confidence:** High

---

## iXBRL (eXtensible Business Reporting Language) mandate

### 2026 requirement

**Effective date:** 1 January 2026

**Applies to:** All corporations (BV/NV) filing annual accounts with KvK.

**Format:** iXBRL is a machine-readable XML-based standard. Accountants and tax software handle this automatically; you do not need to prepare iXBRL manually.

**Impact:** Annual accounts must be filed in iXBRL format (not PDF or Word). Standard accounting software now supports this.

**KVK submission:** Electronic deposit via KvK e-Services portal.

**Confidence:** High

---

## Prinsjesdag (Budget Day) and rule re-versioning

### Annual announcement

**Timing:** Mid-September (typically Tuesday)

**What happens:** Dutch government announces tax rule changes, bracket adjustments, and new deductions/caps for the following fiscal year.

**Action required:** After Prinsjesdag, review all 2027 rule changes and update this folder (05-rules-2026) immediately when 2027 values are announced. Do not wait for calendar year change.

**Examples of typical changes:**
- Zelfstandigenaftrek adjusted up/down
- Box 1 bracket rates revised
- KIA thresholds increased
- MKB winstvrijstelling percentage changed

**Confidence:** High (historical pattern)

## Document retention deadline

| Document type | Retention period | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| Invoices, receipts | 7 years (art. 52 AWR) | Day 7 of retention = destruction date |
| VAT records | 7 years | Same as above |
| Business contracts | 7 years | Same as above |
| Payroll, HR records | 7 years | Same as above |

**Calculation:** Day 1 = date of transaction (or invoice date). Day 2555 = destruction date (approx. 7 years later).

**Audit risk:** If Belastingdienst audits your records, missing documents from prior 7 years are grounds for assessment disputes.

**Confidence:** High

## KOR (small business exemption) annual check

| Action | Timing |
|---|---|
| Calculate annual turnover | End of tax year (Dec 31) |
| If ≤ €20,000 | Remain VAT-exempt next year |
| If > €20,000 | Must register for VAT; applies current year onwards |

**Register before:** Not required if discovered after year-end, but must register effective 1 Jan of next year if threshold exceeded in prior year.

**Confidence:** High

---

## Urencriterium (hours log) deadline

| Requirement | Timing |
|---|---|
| Log business hours | Throughout tax year |
| Retain proof (calendar, timesheet, invoices) | Until 7-year retention expires |
| Declare on tax return if deductions claimed | By filing deadline (31 May) |

**Evidence:** Belastingdienst expects contemporaneous logs (timesheets, calendar entries, invoices with dates). Reconstructed logs after year-end are viewed skeptically.

**Confidence:** High

---

## Representatiekosten and relatiegeschenken annual limits

| Limit | Threshold | Check timing |
|---|---|---|
| Representatiekosten | €5,700 annual cap | End of year; adjust in tax return |
| Relatiegeschenken per recipient | €227 annual limit | Track per recipient; file receipt documentation |

**If exceeded:** Excess is non-deductible; redeclare on tax return or suppletie if discovered mid-year.

**Confidence:** High

## Payment deadlines (if tax is owed)

| Tax type | Deadline | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Income tax (assessed) | 30 days after assessment notice | May request installment plan |
| Corporate tax (Vpb) | 30 days after assessment notice | Same as above |
| VAT (quarterly return) | 30 days after return submission | Can be offset by prior refunds |

**Automatic payment:** If you have a Dutch bank account, Belastingdienst may deduct via incasso (automatic collection). Agree in advance.

**Confidence:** High

---

## Penalty and interest accrual

| Scenario | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Late VAT return | Interest ~6–8% p.a.; admin fine 5–10% of unpaid amount |
| Late income tax return | Interest ~6–8% p.a.; admin penalty €100–€500 (discretionary) |
| Incorrect invoice VAT | Disallowed deduction + interest + potential fraud penalty |
| Suppletie > 8 weeks late | Interest accrues from discovery date; may face audit |

**Interest calculation:** Simple interest, accrues daily from due date until payment received.

**Confidence:** High

---

## Tax year reconciliation

### Estimated vs. actual

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| During tax year | Pay provisional/quarterly taxes or VAT |
| By 31 May next year | File annual return (true-up) |
| After assessment | Receive notice of any refund or additional tax due |

**Refund timing:** If you are owed a refund, Belastingdienst processes it within 6–12 weeks of assessment.

**Confidence:** High
