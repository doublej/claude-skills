# WBSO (R&D Wage Credit) + Innovatiebox (9% IP Tax Rate) — 2026

**Definition:** WBSO is a refundable wage tax credit for R&D personnel; Innovatiebox is a 9% effective corporate tax rate on qualifying IP profits. Both require S&O (Onderzoeks & Ontwikkeling) nexus. **Accountant + RVO mandatory.**

## 2026 Numbers (high confidence)

| Scheme | Rate/Benefit | Scope | Type |
|--------|--------------|-------|------|
| WBSO | Year-versioned % (2026 TBD, historically 32–35%) | Wage costs for R&D staff | Refundable credit |
| Innovatiebox | 9% effective VPB rate | Qualifying IP profit (patents, software, licenses) | Corporate tax reduction |

**Note:** WBSO percentage for 2026 not yet published by RVO; consult current RVO website or accountant.

## WBSO (R&D Wage Credit)

### Eligibility

- **Who:** ZZP with S&O projects, BV with R&D department
- **What:** Qualifying research & development wages, approved S&O statement
- **Thresholds:** Minimum wage spend (varies by year; ~€25k–€50k typical), starter status optional bonus
- **Application:** Annual RVO application, requires S&O documentation and wage records

### Detection Logic

1. Identify projects classified as S&O (novel research, experimentation, technical risk)
2. Separate S&O wages from non-R&D payroll (e.g., sales, admin wages excluded)
3. Estimate qualifying wage cost (only direct labor; not overhead or materials)
4. Verify project documentation (technical specs, experiment logs, proof of risk)
5. Cross-check against RVO eligibility criteria (annually updated)

### Pitfalls

- **Misclassification:** Routine software development does NOT qualify; only novel/high-risk technical innovation
- **Wage/salary isolation:** Accountant must split payroll; mixed roles require allocation
- **Documentation weakness:** Vague project descriptions, missing experiment logs → RVO rejection
- **Starter bonus overlap:** If claiming startersaftrek + WBSO, conditions may conflict; check compatibility
- **Application timing:** Annual deadline (typically Feb/March following the fiscal year); late submission = loss of benefit

## Innovatiebox (9% IP Tax Rate)

### Eligibility

- **Who:** BV (corporate entity) only; ZZP cannot use Innovatiebox
- **What:** Qualifying IP profit from patents, software, trademarks, trade secrets, licenses, know-how
- **Nexus:** Must prove development/acquisition link between innovation and profit (IP nexus test)
- **Minimum profit:** €25,000 typical threshold (varies); check current rules
- **Documentation:** S&O statement, IP registry, development logs, arm's-length transfer pricing

### Detection Logic

1. Identify IP-generating activities (software development, algorithm research, patent creation)
2. Separate IP profit from routine business income
3. Assess "nexus" to qualifying innovation (developed in-house vs. acquired vs. licensed)
4. Verify transfer pricing if IP sold within group
5. Cross-check against Belastingdienst nexus criteria (high judgment required)

### Pitfalls

- **Nexus test failure:** Most common reason for Innovatiebox denial. Must prove causal link between R&D spend and resulting IP.
- **Transfer pricing risk:** If IP transferred between related parties, transfer price must be arm's-length; audited closely.
- **Mixed-income allocation:** Distinguishing pure IP profit (eligible for 9%) from routine services (standard VPB rate 20.5%) is subjective.
- **Small-taxpayer cap:** Different thresholds apply to large vs. small taxpayers (see RVO table; affects maximum benefit).
- **ZZP exclusion:** Cannot claim as sole proprietor; must form BV to access Innovatiebox.
- **Annual documentation:** Must maintain S&O statement and IP register annually; failure to do so forfeits benefit.

## Combined WBSO + Innovatiebox Strategy

**Typical stack:** WBSO covers wages for R&D team → Innovatiebox covers profit from resulting IP. Both schemes support innovation planning.

**Warning:** WBSO and Innovatiebox have separate compliance burdens. Accountant must coordinate:
- WBSO: RVO annual application with wage detail
- Innovatiebox: Transfer pricing, IP nexus documentation, Belastingdienst submission

**Risk level:** High. Misclassification or weak documentation → both schemes audited and disallowed together.
